Welcome to UniversitiesWiki — a growing vision to help school students discover their future, understand the opportunities around them, and find the right path toward higher education with clarity and confidence.

## Our Vision

At UniversitiesWiki, we believe that every student deserves a clear view of the future. Too many young people grow up without knowing what universities exist, what they offer, what careers they lead to, and what opportunities are available to them.

Our mission is to help school kids:

- see a vision of what is possible
- understand the world of universities and careers
- learn about different study paths and institutions
- discover what they are best suited for
- find guidance, information, and inspiration early enough to make stronger decisions

We want to make it easy for students to explore universities, compare options, and understand their chances — all in one place.

## What We Are Building

We are creating a central platform that brings together important information about universities in a simple and student-friendly way.

Our long-term goal is to build:

- a main site that highlights top universities and their strengths
- a clear overview of what students can expect from each institution
- information about admission opportunities, programs, and career paths
- detailed pages for selected universities and institutions
- a knowledge hub that helps students make informed choices

This includes showcasing the top 10 universities and giving short, clear descriptions of what they offer, what makes them unique, and what opportunities they can unlock for students.

## Why This Matters

Many students are unsure where to begin. They may not know:

- which universities are best for their interests
- what courses exist
- how to compare institutions
- what opportunities are available after graduation
- what path matches their strengths and goals

UniversitiesWiki exists to close that gap. We want to turn confusion into direction, and uncertainty into vision.

## Roadmap

### Phase 1: Foundation
- Create a central informational site
- Highlight major universities and their profiles
- Present easy-to-understand summaries for students

### Phase 2: Deep Dive
- Add detailed pages for individual universities
- Share course offerings, strengths, and student opportunities
- Explain what students can expect from each institution

### Phase 3: Expansion
- Build more university-specific pages
- Improve search, comparisons, and navigation
- Grow a helpful, student-first educational resource

## Tech Stack

We are building this vision with a modern, scalable, and accessible web stack. Our planned and used technologies include:

- HTML5 for structure and content
- CSS3 for design and responsive layouts
- JavaScript for interaction and functionality
- React for user interfaces and reusable components
- Next.js for modern web application development
- GitHub Pages / static hosting for simple public deployment
- Markdown for documentation and content structure
- GitHub for collaboration, version control, and publishing

This combination allows us to build a clean, fast, and beginner-friendly experience while keeping the project easy to scale over time.

## Community and Impact

UniversitiesWiki is more than just a website — it is a mission to support the future of students. We want to help young people see what is possible and understand the opportunities that exist around them.

We aim to:

- make university information more accessible
- help students explore paths that fit their dreams
- support better educational decisions
- create a website ecosystem that grows with time
